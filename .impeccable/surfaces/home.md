# Surface: Home

**Mode:** Experience  
**Route:** `/` (`src/app/page.tsx`)

## Scope

Single-page portfolio for a senior backend engineer. Visitors evaluate technical credibility through case studies, stack depth, and experience — not visual novelty.

## Audience & job

Hiring managers and tech leads deciding whether to interview or hire. Job: assess systems thinking, .NET depth, and delivery track record in under two minutes.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The page reads like an engineering postmortem index — dense, asymmetric, monospace-accented — refusing the centered-hero portfolio template.

**OWN-WORLD:** Warm off-white ground (OKLCH), near-black ink, single muted teal accent on links only. Schibsted Grotesk body/display, JetBrains Mono for tags and metadata. Hairline rules, no card chrome, no gradients.

**STORY:** Visitor sees who this engineer is, scans 3–4 systems case studies with architecture and metrics, checks stack honesty, skims timeline, copies contact links.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Name and title occupy left two-thirds at display scale; one-line positioning statement and current role sit in a right-offset column with a vertical rule between. Sticky section nav anchors left edge below fold on desktop.

**FORM:** Asymmetric editorial grid (code-led). Seed: user-pinned brief, no concept roll.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
